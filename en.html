Approach

Finding the idea behind the brand

I'm a bilingual copywriter with a background in Communication Studies. I think like a creative strategist — my job isn't just to write, it's to find the idea underneath your brand and put it into words.

"I combine research, strategic thinking and writing to create communication that feels relevant rather than simply 'marketable.'"

I'm particularly interested in finding the idea behind a piece of content: why it should exist, what it should communicate, and how it can make people stop, think or feel something.

In practice, that means a three-step process I repeat on every project: first I research the brand, its audience and its competitors; then I define a clear concept or positioning; only then do I write — because good copy without strategy behind it is just a nice sentence.

Case study 01

CORE Training Center

IndustryGym in its early days
ServiceContent strategy

Context

CORE was a newly opened training center that needed to become known in its neighborhood to attract more members.

Objective

Position the brand around integral physical and mental wellbeing — not drastic physical transformation — and build a sense of community around group training.

Target audience

  • Professionals aged 20–40 looking for a moment to unwind within their weekly routine.
  • Informal workers aged 20–40 aiming to stay healthy despite a demanding work schedule.
  • Older adults looking to stay active.

Strategy

I defined a close, friendly tone of voice, treating followers as members of a community rather than customers. The content plan prioritized fewer fixed posts and more stories, aiming to increase real interaction (reposts) over posting volume, and improved highlight covers with value-driven resources (nutrition, energy, exercise).

Brand keywords

Training center · Physical & mental wellbeing · Group training · Movement is health · Personalized training · Community in motion

Case study 02

Espacio Conectar

IndustryHolistic kinesiology
ServiceContent strategy

Context

Espacio Conectar wanted to show their work in a more organic, human way — moving away from purely "marketing-driven" communication. Their social presence didn't reflect the reputation they already had among patients.

Objective

Position the practice as a reference in holistic kinesiology, foregrounding real human accompaniment over technique, and generate content that conveyed warmth, trust and empathy.

Strategy

I developed a mission, vision and a detailed audience map (motivations, frustrations, consumption habits), along with a content plan organized by type, each with its own posting frequency:

  • Testimonials: patient stories, first-person or narrated by the practice.
  • Educational: holistic kinesiology tips and techniques explained in plain language.
  • Inspirational: reflections and pieces conveying wellbeing and support.
  • Institutional: introducing the professionals, values and vision.
  • Community: patient engagement and health-related dates.

Copy examples

"Behind every pain, there's a story that deserves to be heard."
"Your story matters, your wellbeing does too."

Case study 03

Obras SM

IndustryPersonal brand — Master Building Foreman
ServiceContent strategy + goals

Context

Silvina Monroy, a Master Building Foreman, needed more projects. Her online presence didn't reflect her track record or her integral approach to construction and interior design, and she was losing time on free quotes that rarely converted into clients.

Objective

Position Obras SM as a full-service construction and interior design studio — showing her potential beyond past work alone.

Strategy

I designed an institutional and educational content plan, with client testimonials and an online advisory service (with an intake template) to capitalize on quotes that were previously going to waste. I set staged growth goals:

  • Months 1–3: institutional content and channel professionalization — weekly stories, service overviews, brand story.
  • Months 3–7: doubling down on what works, aiming for at least one viral piece, focus on retention.
  • Month 10+: building community and, if engagement keeps climbing, investing in paid media.

Featured story — "Deco" series

Result — first week of applying the strategy

profile views in 30 days (~5.6x), with followers also trending up.

Case study 04

ASEA — #CreáFuturo

ClientArgentina's Entrepreneurs Association
ServiceCampaign concept + copy

The most challenging project of my career so far.

Context

ASEA needed to promote the second edition of the National Entrepreneurs' Survey nationwide — an initiative to map the real experience of entrepreneurship across Argentina.

Objective

Get entrepreneurs across the country to take the survey, reinforcing their role in shaping public policy for the entrepreneurial ecosystem.

The concept

#CreáFuturo — "Your experience creates the future."

I wrote the full campaign concept and adapted it across formats:

  • Media kit for distribution
  • Copy for Instagram, LinkedIn and X
  • WhatsApp message for direct outreach
  • Email marketing
  • Launch presentation

The campaign is still live.
